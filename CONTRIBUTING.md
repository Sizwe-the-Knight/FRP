# Contributing to Free-Range Picks

FRP explains independent software and technology to people who do not necessarily know the jargon. Useful, sourced suggestions matter more than hype.

The repository and contact destinations are not published yet. The local [contribution page](src/pages/contribute.astro) reflects that honestly. Future GitHub Issue Forms are prepared in [.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/); they do not send anything until an approved repository exists.

For a discovery, include the official project/repository URL, what it helps someone do, your relationship to the project and sources for cost, licence, requirements and limitations. For corrections, include the article URL, the incorrect statement and an official source supporting the change. Do not submit personal system reports, credentials or private contact details in public issues.

Submissions receive manual review and may be declined. There is no automatic publication, paid placement, guaranteed response time or implied endorsement. Do not copy a developer's README or send assets you cannot authorize us to use. Follow a project's security-reporting process for vulnerabilities.

For editorial changes, use the [publishing guide](docs/publishing.md). Run `npm run check`, `npm test` and `npm run build`. Keep drafts unpublished and preserve permanent slugs. No database, accounts or external AI service is needed.
