## FRP opinion

“Free” can answer the price question while leaving the effort question open. We think a good recommendation should explain both.

For example, [Shotcut's FAQ](https://www.shotcut.org/FAQ/) separates the demands of SD, HD and 4K editing. [Wan2.1](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.1) documents a model-specific video-memory figure. Those are different kinds of projects; a free download is not evidence that either fits every computer.

Before choosing, ask what you want to finish, what equipment you have and how much setup you want to learn. That is editorial judgement, not a ranking or a performance study.
